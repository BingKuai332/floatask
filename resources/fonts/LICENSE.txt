MiSans.ttf
  Source: https://hyperos.mi.com/font/ (Xiaomi HyperOS MiSans;
  mirrored at https://statics.kimi.ai/neo-design-static/p-font/MiSans.ttf)

The application never downloads fonts at runtime.
